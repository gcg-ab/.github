### AI where it helps. Simpler software where it doesn’t.

We help companies in Sweden automate routine work with AI, run reliable cloud systems and build web and mobile apps. Alongside client work, we build our own software products.

**What we do**

- **AI & automation.** Entering invoice and order data into your systems, sorting incoming email and support tickets, and answering staff questions from your own documents.
- **Cloud solutions.** New environments, moves from your own servers, automated deployments and monitoring, and reviews of cost and security on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
- **App development.** New web and mobile apps for your customers or your own staff.
- **Systems & integrations.** Features, backend services and APIs inside the systems you already have, and connections between your tools.

**How we work**

1. We learn how your business works and the limits we have to work within.
2. We recommend the solution that fits best, and tell you when a simpler option or no AI at all is the better choice.
3. We build it with you and help maintain it after launch.
